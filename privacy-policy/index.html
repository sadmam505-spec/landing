Privacy Policy

Gritorax Team

Welcome to Gritorax. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our platform. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us. This includes:

  • Technical Data: Your Telegram User ID.
  • Interaction Data: Information regarding the tasks you complete, points or rewards tracked, and navigation habits on our site.
  • Communication Data: Any messages or feedback you send to our support team via Telegram.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and provide our gaming and reward services.
  • To improve platform performance and user experience.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access to our system.
  • To communicate updates, reward notifications, or support responses.

3. Data Protection and Security

We take the security of your data seriously. We implement industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction.

4. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to external sites or third-party applications (e.g., Telegram). Please note that we do not control the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before interacting with them.

5. Cookies Policy

We may use cookies to understand your preferences and improve your future experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, although some features of our platform might not function correctly if you do so.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as it is necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, such as maintaining reward records and providing support. Once your data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

7. Children's Privacy

Gritorax is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to our official support channel via Telegram.
